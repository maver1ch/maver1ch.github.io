## Problem

ESG disclosure for Vietnamese banks has moved from a PR exercise to a hard compliance and capital-markets metric — but the reports themselves are brutal to query.

- **Heterogeneous PDFs**: 28–80 page sustainability reports with multi-column layouts, embedded tables, mixed heading hierarchies, and scanned figures.
- **Vocabulary mismatch**: local Vietnamese framing rarely aligns one-to-one with GRI Universal Standards (200 / 300 / 400 series), so lexical retrieval falls over.
- **Hallucination risk**: standard RAG invents numbers or misattributes them across companies and years — unacceptable when an answer is going to a regulator or an analyst.
- **Multi-hop reasoning**: real analyst questions cross sections (e.g. green credit balance × scope-1 emissions × board-diversity ratios) — single-shot vector search can't compose the answer.

The system has two layers. The **offline** layer (Modules 1–3) extracts GRI metadata, segments bank reports and builds the **Neo4j graph**. The **online** layer (Modules 4–5) answers questions with **KG-driven retrieval**, then checks each answer with **automated fact-checking**.

<ArchitectureSection
  step="02"
  title="Module 1 — GRI Metadata Extraction Pipeline"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/2-metadata-extraction.png"
  alt="GRI metadata extraction pipeline — PyMuPDF parsing followed by LLM-driven semantic extraction into a 5-tuple structured record"

>
**PyMuPDF** parses the official GRI standards (Series 200, 300, 400) into chunks. An LLM with **few-shot ESG examples** turns each disclosure into a **5-tuple**: category, indicator, title, disclosure and detail. This becomes the **controlled vocabulary** every later module aligns to.
</ArchitectureSection>

<ArchitectureSection
  step="03"
  title="Module 2 — ESG Reports Preprocessing"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/3-report-processing.png"
  alt="ESG reports preprocessing pipeline — OLMoCR linearization, RAP and ALIGN ToC anchoring, Qwen3-4B sliding-window segmentation, hierarchical labeling"
  challenge="Bank reports are 28–80 page PDFs with multi-column layouts, tables and mixed headings."
>
**OLMoCR** turns chaotic layouts and tables into ordered text. RAP + ALIGN anchors headings to the text. **Qwen3-4B** with a sliding window finds **semantic breakpoints**, not fixed-size chunks, and each segment gets a **4-level label**: category, GRI indicator, statement type, sentiment.
</ArchitectureSection>

<ArchitectureSection
  step="04"
  title="Module 3 — ESG Knowledge Graph in Neo4j"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/4-knowledge-graph.png"
  alt="ESG knowledge graph construction — 5 node types and relational edges including HAS_REPORT, CONTAINS, RELATED_TO, BELONGS_TO"

Neo4j holds **5 node types** (Company, Report, ESG_Statement, GRI_Indicator, ESG_Category) linked by edges like HAS_REPORT and BELONGS_TO. Each statement node stores its **vector embedding** and **provenance** (company, year, page). This allows graph traversal and fully source-traceable answers.
</ArchitectureSection>

<ArchitectureSection
  step="05"
  title="Module 4 — KG-Driven Hybrid RAG"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/5-kg-rag.png"
  alt="KG-driven retrieval-augmented generation pipeline — question understanding, Cypher candidate generation, dual-stream scoring, hybrid evidence fusion, provenance-grounded generation"
  highlight
  challenge="Analyst questions cross sections, and a single top-K vector lookup cannot compose the answer."
>
The question is classified, then an LLM turns it into a **Cypher query** on Neo4j. **Dual-stream scoring** (semantic + keyword) ranks the candidates; top results are fused and deduped. The answer carries **full provenance**, so every sentence traces to its source.
</ArchitectureSection>

<PhaseMarker label="Phase 3 — Reliability: Automated Fact-Checking" highlight />

<ArchitectureSection
  step="06"
  title="Module 5 — Fact-Checking Pipeline Overview"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/6-fact-checking.png"
  alt="Six-stage automated fact-checking pipeline — atomic claim extraction, query generation, hybrid retrieval, rerank and distill, LLM-as-judge, aggregation"
  challenge="Standard RAG can invent numbers or mix them up across companies and years."
>
Each answer is split into **atomic claims**. Every claim gets its own queries, **hybrid retrieval**, reranking and an **LLM-as-judge** verdict. Results combine into a verification report of claims, verdicts and citations. The report is something a compliance team can show a regulator.
</ArchitectureSection>

<ArchitectureSection
  step="07"
  title="Hybrid Retrieval & Cross-Encoder Reranking"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/7-retrieval-reranking.png"
  alt="Stage 3-4 evidence construction — parallel hybrid retrieval from internal BM25 + dense FAISS and external web search, then deduplication, cross-encoder reranking, and top-K distillation"
  challenge="Bi-encoder retrieval misses numeric agreement, negation and unit mismatches."
>
Each claim's queries search two channels in parallel: **internal** BM25 + dense **FAISS** over the private ESG corpus (top-5 each), and **external** web search (top-5). Results are merged and deduped, keeping the max score. A **cross-encoder** then filters by threshold and keeps the top-K as final evidence.
</ArchitectureSection>

<ArchitectureSection
  step="08"
  title="Verdicts, Confidence Scoring, Review-Trigger"
  image="/assets/case-studies/esg-business-report/8-verdict-result-aggregation.png"
  alt="Verdict aggregation — LLM-as-a-judge assigns FEVER-style labels (SUPPORTS / NOT_ENOUGH_INFO / REFUTES), then signed and overall confidence is computed and a manual review flag fires below threshold"
  highlight
  challenge="In regulated work, a low-confidence answer must never ship silently."
>
An **LLM-as-judge** labels each claim SUPPORTS (+1), NOT_ENOUGH_INFO (0) or REFUTES (-1), FEVER-style, with a confidence score. Labels combine into a **signed confidence in [-1, 1]**: positive means support, negative means refutation. Below a set threshold, a **manual-review flag** fires.
</ArchitectureSection>

## Outcome

- **88.14% overall answer accuracy** on the expert-annotated benchmark (KG_Enhanced + Qwen3-8B) — up from 69.57% on baseline KG and 53.65% on BM25.
- **+30 points Recall@3** vs BM25 baseline (83.0% vs 52.5%); KG-aware retrieval is the lever, cross-encoder reranking is the multiplier.
- **Expert-validated benchmark**: 1,440 question-document pairs (120 questions × 12 reports) annotated by ESG domain experts. Balanced across Environmental (45.8%), Social (26.7%), and Governance (27.5%) — designed to stress-test the system, not flatter it.
- **Claim-level reliability layer**: FEVER-style verdicts with signed confidence in [-1, 1] and a manual-review trigger below threshold — production-grade pattern for regulated domains where silent failure is unacceptable.
- **Provenance to the page**: every answer traceable to *company, year, page, and GRI indicator*. Non-negotiable for ESG analytics, audit, and regulator-facing workflows.
